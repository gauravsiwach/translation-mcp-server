## Problem Statement

Hello everyone.
As organizations expand into new markets, localization becomes increasingly difficult. Every new market adds new languages, more translations, and greater coordination overhead.
Today, localization is often managed through spreadsheets, translation tools, and manual workflows. As scale increases, updates become slower, reviews become harder to track, and localization turns into a delivery bottleneck.
At PepsiConnect B2B alone, we support more than 17 markets today, with many more on the roadmap.

## Solution Statement
So we asked a simple question:
What if localization was as easy as having a conversation?

That's exactly what we built: LocaleFlow MCP Agent — an AI-powered localization platform that enables developers, product managers, and market teams to create, translate, review, and manage localization content using natural language.
LocaleFlow combines three capabilities:
AI-generated translations across all supported locales.
Human-in-the-loop review and approval workflows.
Design-aware context through Figma integration.
The result is faster localization, lower operational overhead, and a process that scales with the business.
